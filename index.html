About Us

REECS 1Energy Corporation is a DTI-registered energy consultancy firm established in 2016 by a team of seasoned professionals with extensive experience in power system operations and the electricity market. REECS provides specialized expertise in transmission and distribution system operations and maintenance, grid capacity assessment, power system studies, energy market operations, billing and settlement, WESM BCQ declaration, variable renewable energy (VRE) forecasting, demand-side management, demand aggregation, grid compliance testing, and renewable energy project development.
The firm supports Distribution Utilities, Electric Cooperatives, Generation Companies, Contestable Customers, and other grid-connected entities through technical and non-technical advisory services, financial projections, and business and commercial modeling.
Backed by a strong project track record and a highly competent team, REECS is committed to building long-term partnerships by delivering reliable, cost-effective, and high-quality consultancy services.
As a responsible energy consultancy, REECS fully supports the government's initiatives to accelerate the adoption of clean and renewable energy, contributing to a sustainable and decarbonized energy future.
